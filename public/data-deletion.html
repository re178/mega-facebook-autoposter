Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your personal data from VIRALOOP, you have two options:

Option 1: Self-Service Deletion

Log into your VIRALOOP account and navigate to Account Settings → Delete Account. This will permanently remove:

  • Your account profile and authentication data
  • Connected Facebook pages and tokens
  • Scheduled posts and AI topics
  • Activity logs and message history

⚠️ Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your data will be removed within 30 days.

Option 2: Request by Email

Send an email to privacy@viraloop.com with:

  • Subject line: "DATA DELETION REQUEST"
  • Your registered email address
  • Confirmation that you own the account

We will process your request within 7 business days and confirm deletion via email.

Facebook App Integration

If you connected your Facebook account through VIRALOOP, you can also remove the integration directly from Facebook:

  • Go to Facebook Settings → Business Integrations
  • Find VIRALOOP in the list
  • Click "Remove" to revoke access
Note: Some data may remain in backups for security and compliance purposes for a limited period. This data is not accessible in active systems.
