Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 15, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help us remember your preferences and keep your session secure.

How We Use Cookies

We use only essential cookies necessary for platform functionality:

  • Session Cookies: Keep you logged in during your visit
  • Security Cookies: Protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your sidebar state and UI preferences

Third-Party Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you connect third-party services (Facebook), they may set their own cookies subject to their policies.

Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, our platform requires essential cookies for authentication and will not function properly if disabled.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
