Community Guidelines

VIRALOOP is committed to providing a safe, respectful platform for all users. These guidelines apply to your use of our platform and any content you publish through connected services.

Respect Others

  • No hate speech, harassment, or bullying
  • No threats, intimidation, or abusive behavior
  • No discriminatory content targeting race, religion, gender, or orientation

Post Responsible Content

  • No spam, deceptive content, or misleading information
  • No illegal activities or promoting harmful products
  • No impersonation or fake accounts
  • Review AI-generated content before publishing

Follow Platform Rules

  • Comply with Facebook, Instagram, and other connected platform policies
  • Do not manipulate engagement metrics or use bots
  • Respect intellectual property rights

Account Responsibility

  • Keep your credentials secure
  • Report security issues immediately
  • Do not share API tokens or access keys

Consequences of Violation

Violating these guidelines may result in:

  • Warning and temporary feature restrictions
  • Suspension of automation capabilities
  • Permanent account termination
  • Reporting to relevant platforms

Reporting Violations

To report a violation, contact: viraloopsupport@gmail.com
